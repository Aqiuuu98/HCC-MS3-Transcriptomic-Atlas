# Frozen study design

The discovery–validation–perturbation study links an exploratory TCGA-LIHC prognostic phenotype to risk-blind malignant-cell states in GSE149614. Original Top10 discovery used historical bulk exploration; M01 fitting was restricted to 238 development patients, with conditional internal holdout evaluation in 102 patients. Original RiskScore (365 patients) and M01 are different objects.

The final 12,057 lineage-clean malignant cells in five patients are partitioned into MS1–MS8. MS3 (720 cells) is the primary bulk-risk-concordant proliferative/mitotic state; Core50 is frozen, Core100 is sensitivity. MS6 is the differentiated/metabolic comparator. Patient-aware conservation and within-discovery LOPO do not constitute external clinical validation.

Step27 PAGA/pseudotime and Step28 sensitivity describe trajectory-compatible organization, without velocity or lineage proof. Step29 frozen motif-filtered regulons and Step30 prior-constrained communication support state-associated architecture, not causality.

GSE326201 (10 patients, 4,688 malignant cells) transfers frozen programs for biological validation. GSE281759 (17 sections in four patients, 27,224 in-tissue spots) evaluates program concordance and heterogeneous reciprocity. Neither is external prognostic survival validation.

Step33 virtually suppresses FOXM1, MYBL2, E2F8, TFDP1 and E2F1 using the frozen GRN. Step34 accepts native CellOracle-derived shifts in frozen 30D Harmony, with movement toward MS5 as primary geometry; the expression-to-Harmony surrogate is REJECTED. Step35 reuses frozen L1000FWD Q1/Q2/Q3 responses and ranks; eight final Tier A/B candidates remain unchanged. Computational suppression/reversal does not establish therapeutic efficacy or state reprogramming.
